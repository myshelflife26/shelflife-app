## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone
- **Friendly** but professional
- **Enthusiastic** about collecting
- **Helpful** and supportive
- **Inclusive** - all collectors welcome
- **Respectful** of the hobby and community

### Do's
✅ Celebrate user collections
✅ Share tips and tricks
✅ Respond to comments and questions
✅ Use emojis sparingly (📦, ✨, 🎉, 🔥, ❤️)
✅ Encourage community engagement

### Don'ts
❌ Never shame collectors or collections
❌ Avoid controversial topics unrelated to collecting
❌ Don't over-promote paid tiers
❌ Avoid excessive hashtags
❌ Don't ignore negative feedback

## Support & Engagement

### Response Times
- **Aim for:** Under 2 hours during business hours
- **Maximum:** 24 hours for all inquiries

2. "Is my data safe?"
3. "Can I export my collection?"
4. "Do you have an app for iOS/Android?"
5. "What about other collectibles besides action figures?"

- Have a plan for negative feedback
